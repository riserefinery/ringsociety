## 3. How We Share Personal Information

We do not rent personal information or sell personal information for money. However, privacy laws may define “sale,” “share,” or similar terms broadly. The disclosures described below—especially advertising technology disclosures and some lead-matching or data-enrichment activities—may be treated as a sale, sharing, targeted-advertising disclosure, or similar processing under some state laws. Your choices are explained in Sections 8 and 9.

### A. Independent jewelers matched through Ring Society

If you complete a quiz or submit information to receive a local jeweler match, request that a jeweler contact you, or book an appointment with a jeweler through our Services, we may disclose the limited information reasonably necessary to facilitate that request to the matched independent jeweler. This may include your name, mobile or other phone number, general location, and appointment details. **Ring Society does not disclose your email address or detailed quiz responses to a matched jeweler unless you separately and expressly direct us to do so.**

Matched jewelers are independent businesses. Ring Society requires participating jewelers to use Ring Society-provided personal information only to respond to the requested match, consultation, appointment, or related service and not to add you to their own marketing lists or use your information for their independent marketing. Ring Society retains control of the consumer record, including email and detailed quiz data. A matched jeweler’s use of information after it receives the information may also be governed by its own privacy notice and applicable law.

### B. Service providers and contractors

We disclose personal information to companies that perform services for us or on our behalf, including providers of hosting, website functionality, analytics, advertising, CRM and customer-service tools, scheduling, chat, email, SMS/MMS delivery, data storage, security, data enrichment, address matching, direct-mail fulfillment, professional services, and similar operational support. These recipients are authorized to process personal information only as appropriate to perform services for us or as otherwise permitted by law.

### C. Advertising, analytics, and social-media partners

We use analytics and advertising technologies, including cookies, pixels, tags, software-development kits, and similar tools. These tools may disclose online identifiers, device information, and online activity to analytics, advertising, and social-media partners so that we can measure performance, deliver or limit advertising, and understand how visitors use the Services. Depending on the applicable law and configuration, this may be considered “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising or another regulated disclosure.

### D. Enrichment, identity-resolution, and direct-mail providers

Where permitted by law, we may use service providers to verify, supplement, or associate information that you provide—such as matching an email address, postal address, phone number, device, or other identifier with other contact or household information. We may use that information to improve our records, prevent fraud, evaluate the relevance of our Services, and send direct mail or other permitted communications. We will not use a newly implemented enrichment or matching provider in a manner materially inconsistent with this Policy without updating this Policy or providing any notice required by law.

### E. Legal, safety, and corporate disclosures

We may disclose personal information when we reasonably believe it is necessary to comply with law; respond to lawful legal process; protect the security, rights, property, or safety of Ring Society, our users, or others; investigate fraud or abuse; or enforce our agreements.
