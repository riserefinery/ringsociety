**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 1, 2026

Ring Society is a trade name of **ShopFine LLC** (“**Ring Society**,” “**we**,” “**us**,” or “**our**”). By receiving, activating, using, or attempting to redeem a Diamond Card, you agree to these Diamond Card Terms, Ring Society’s [Terms & Conditions](/terms-and-conditions), and [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy). If there is a conflict between these Diamond Card Terms and Ring Society’s general Terms & Conditions, these Diamond Card Terms control for the Diamond Card offer.

> **The Diamond Card is a promotional savings offer, not a credit card, stored-value card, gift certificate, or cash-equivalent instrument.**

## 1. Overview and Availability

The Diamond Card may provide an eligible consumer with a discount toward a qualifying purchase of an engagement ring or wedding band from an independent, participating jeweler matched through Ring Society.

The offer is subject to local availability. Ring Society will provide or send a Diamond Card only when we have identified a certified, participating jeweler in your local area that is able to honor the offer. A quiz result, jeweler match request, or form submission does not guarantee that a Diamond Card, a jeweler match, appointment availability, or particular discount will be available.

The Diamond Card is offered to eligible consumers located in the United States. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must participate in any consultation and any qualifying purchase. The offer is void where prohibited by law.

## 2. How to Activate and Redeem Your Diamond Card

To activate the Diamond Card, you must book a free consultation with your matched participating jeweler within **seven (7) days** after the Diamond Card is issued or made available to you.

Once activated, you have **sixty (60) days** from the activation date to attend your consultation in person and use the Diamond Card toward a qualifying purchase. The Diamond Card is redeemable only in person at the store of your matched participating jeweler. It cannot be redeemed online, by telephone, or through another jeweler unless Ring Society and the matched participating jeweler expressly confirm otherwise in writing.

The Diamond Card must be presented or otherwise verified at the time of the qualifying purchase. The matched jeweler may require reasonable identification, the original message or card, confirmation of the match or appointment, and other information reasonably necessary to verify eligibility and prevent fraud.

| Step | Timeframe | Requirement |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Receive or unlock your Diamond Card** | Subject to local availability | Ring Society must have a certified, participating jeweler available in your area. |
| **Activate your Diamond Card** | Within 7 days | Book a free consultation with your matched participating jeweler. |
| **Use your Diamond Card** | Within 60 days after activation | Attend the consultation and complete a qualifying purchase in person at the matched jeweler’s store. |

## 3. Diamond Card Savings Amounts

The Diamond Card value is based on the final pre-Diamond-Card price of the qualifying ring purchase shown on the matched jeweler’s sales order. The following savings tiers apply:

| Qualifying ring purchase price | Diamond Card savings |
| ----: | ----: |
| $2,500 or more | $250 off |
| $5,000 or more | $500 off |
| $10,000 or more | $1,000 off |
| $15,000 or more | $1,500 off |
| $20,000 or more | $2,000 off |

Most matched couples receive **$500 to $1,500 off**. Actual savings depend on the qualifying purchase price, your local participating jeweler, product availability, and the terms of the offer at the time of redemption.

The Diamond Card discount is applied once to the qualifying transaction. It does not alter the jeweler’s independently set product price, does not create a price guarantee, and cannot be used to claim a discount that exceeds the applicable Diamond Card tier.

## 4. Eligible Purchases and Restrictions

The Diamond Card is valid toward a qualifying purchase of an engagement ring or wedding band from your matched participating jeweler. Some brands, products, designers, and merchandise categories may be excluded at the matched jeweler’s discretion. A participating jeweler may, at its discretion, allow the Diamond Card to be applied to another jewelry purchase, but is not required to do so.

Unless required by law or expressly stated in writing by Ring Society and the matched participating jeweler, the Diamond Card:

| Restriction | What it means |
| :---- | :---- |
| **One-time use** | The Diamond Card may be used once and is exhausted after redemption. |
| **One per person or household** | Ring Society may limit the offer to one Diamond Card per eligible consumer or household. |
| **No cash value** | The Diamond Card is not redeemable for cash, a refund, credit, or stored value. |
| **Not transferable** | The Diamond Card may not be sold, transferred, assigned, or exchanged. |
| **No combining** | The Diamond Card may not be combined with another discount, promotion, financing incentive, or offer. |
| **In-person only** | The Diamond Card cannot be applied online or over the phone. |
| **No retroactive use** | The Diamond Card may not be applied to a purchase completed before the card is activated or presented. |

No purchase is required to take the Ring Society quiz, request a jeweler match, or receive a Diamond Card when one is available. **A qualifying purchase is required to redeem the Diamond Card savings.**

Ring Society or a matched participating jeweler may, but is not obligated to, offer an extension of the time to book a consultation, activate a Diamond Card, attend a consultation, or redeem Diamond Card savings. An extension is valid only when it is expressly offered and recorded by Ring Society or the matched participating jeweler.

An extension is a discretionary promotional courtesy, not a guaranteed benefit or continuing right. Ring Society and participating jewelers may decide whether to offer, deny, limit, modify, or discontinue extensions at any time, subject to applicable law. A marketing message, campaign page, or offer that specifically grants an extension will state the terms of that extension. No extension is created by a consumer’s request, a missed deadline, a previously offered extension, or a general statement that appointments may occur weeks later.

Ring Society may connect you with an independent participating jeweler based on your local area, stated preferences, availability, and other relevant factors. Participating jewelers may support Diamond Card offers because Ring Society connects them with consumers who have expressed interest in an in-person consultation. A jeweler’s participation, inventory, eligibility decisions, pricing, and ability to honor an offer may vary by location and time.
