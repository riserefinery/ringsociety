# Do Not Disclose My Personal Information

**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 1, 2026

Ring Society respects your privacy choices. Use this page to ask us not to disclose your personal information for certain purposes, to opt out of applicable sale, sharing, targeted advertising, data enrichment, or marketing direct mail, or to make another privacy request.

Ring Society is a trade name of **ShopFine LLC**. For more information about our data practices, please read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

> **You do not need a Ring Society account to make a privacy request.** We will not deny you access to our public educational content because you exercise an applicable privacy right.

## Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your state of residence and our processing of your personal information, you may have the right to ask us to take one or more of the actions below.

| Request | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Do not sell or share my personal information** | Ask Ring Society not to engage in applicable disclosures of your personal information that may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under state privacy law. This may include certain advertising-technology disclosures and other nonessential disclosures. |
| **Opt out of targeted advertising** | Ask Ring Society not to use or disclose your personal information for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising, where applicable. |
| **Do not use my information for nonessential enrichment or direct mail** | Ask Ring Society not to use your information for nonessential address matching, identity resolution, data enrichment, or Ring Society marketing direct mail. |
| **Access or know** | Ask what personal information Ring Society has collected about you and how it has been used or disclosed, subject to applicable law. |
| **Correct** | Ask us to correct inaccurate personal information that we maintain about you. |
| **Delete** | Ask us to delete personal information that we collected about you, subject to legal and other applicable exceptions. |
| **Appeal a privacy decision** | Ask us to reconsider a decision about a prior privacy request. |

## Submit a Request

Please complete the privacy-request form below or email us at **hello@ringsociety.com** with the subject line **“Privacy Request.”** To protect your information, we may ask for information reasonably necessary to identify the record associated with your request and verify your identity. We will use verification information only for that purpose.

## Important Information About Your Request

### A jeweler match or appointment you requested

If you submitted information to receive a jeweler match, asked a jeweler to contact you, or booked an appointment, Ring Society may disclose the information reasonably necessary to complete that requested service to the matched independent jeweler. A request made through this page will not automatically cancel a match, appointment, or disclosure that you specifically requested before we received your privacy request.

If you want to cancel a pending jeweler match or appointment, please state that clearly in the “Additional information” field or email **hello@ringsociety.com** with the subject line **“Cancel Jeweler Match or Appointment.”** We will make reasonable efforts to stop a pending disclosure or appointment when we receive the request in time to do so.

### Marketing text messages and emails

This page does not replace a text-message **STOP** request or an email unsubscribe request. To stop Ring Society SMS/MMS messages, reply **STOP** to any Ring Society text. For help, reply **HELP**. To stop marketing email, use the unsubscribe link in the email. We may retain limited information needed to honor your preferences, maintain consent and opt-out records, comply with law, or protect our rights.

Ring Society does not share SMS/MMS originator opt-in data or consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

### Browser-based privacy preferences

Where required by law, Ring Society treats a valid opt-out preference signal, such as the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)**, as a request to opt out of applicable sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising processing for the browser or device sending the signal. A browser-based signal may not apply to information that we associate with you through another browser, device, email address, phone number, or offline interaction. To apply your choice more broadly to Ring Society records, please submit the form or email request above.

### Verification, timing, and appeals

We may need to verify your identity before we provide, correct, or delete personal information, or respond to certain other privacy requests. We will not use verification information for unrelated purposes. We aim to acknowledge and respond to requests within the timeframes required by applicable law. If we need more time, we will let you know when required.

If we deny a request, you may appeal by emailing **hello@ringsociety.com** with the subject line **“Privacy Appeal”** within 60 days of our decision. Please identify the request, explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered, and provide any relevant information.
