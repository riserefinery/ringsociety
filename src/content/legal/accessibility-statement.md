# Accessibility Statement

**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 1, 2026

Ring Society is committed to making its websites, including `ringsociety.com` and `app.ringsociety.com`, and its digital services accessible and usable for all visitors, including people with disabilities. Ring Society is a trade name of **ShopFine LLC**.

We are working to provide a website experience that is inclusive, understandable, and compatible with commonly used assistive technologies. Our accessibility efforts are guided by the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA** as our target standard.

## Our Accessibility Commitment

We aim to design, develop, and maintain our website so that visitors can access information, navigate features, complete forms, request a jeweler match, book an appointment, and contact us using a variety of devices and assistive technologies. Our work includes ongoing efforts to support:

| Accessibility area | Our intended approach |
|---|---|
| **Keyboard access** | Enable meaningful website features and interactive controls to be used with a keyboard. |
| **Screen-reader compatibility** | Use semantic structure, meaningful text alternatives, descriptive labels, and accessible names for relevant content and controls. |
| **Visual presentation** | Use readable text, appropriate color contrast, responsive layouts, and content that can be enlarged without unnecessary loss of functionality. |
| **Forms and interactions** | Provide labels, instructions, error identification, and feedback designed to help visitors complete forms and understand next steps. |
| **Media and content** | Provide captions, transcripts, descriptions, or other accessible alternatives where appropriate and reasonably practicable. |
| **Ongoing quality assurance** | Review new and updated website content and features for accessibility considerations and work to remediate material issues that we identify. |

## Accessibility Is an Ongoing Effort

We recognize that accessibility is an ongoing process. Technology, browsers, assistive tools, website content, and applicable standards evolve over time. Although we strive to meet our accessibility goals, some content, features, or third-party tools may not yet be fully accessible in every circumstance.

We welcome feedback that helps us identify barriers and improve the experience. If you have difficulty accessing any content, feature, form, or service on Ring Society’s website, please let us know. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information, service, or assistance you need through an accessible alternative, where feasible.

## Third-Party Content and Services

Some parts of the website may include or link to content, products, services, widgets, booking tools, chat tools, social-media platforms, or websites operated by independent jewelers or other third parties. Ring Society does not control the accessibility of third-party content or services. If you encounter an accessibility barrier involving a third-party service that is integrated with or linked from our website, please notify us. We will consider the information in our ongoing accessibility efforts and, where feasible, help identify an alternative path to access the Ring Society service.

## Updates to This Statement

We may update this Accessibility Statement to reflect improvements to the website, changes in technology, or changes in our accessibility practices. We will post the updated statement on this page and revise the “Last Updated” date.
